MotoRacer PC Reverse Engineering                           Analysis Note


                 CARAC: THE BIKE CHARACTERISTIC TABLES


Abstract

   CARAC1.BPT through CARAC8.BPT, present in each of the four bike
   banks, are the vehicle parameter tables. All 32 decode completely
   [verified]: 73 values each, one leading integer and the rest IEEE
   floats.

   These are the numbers a faithful reimplementation of the handling
   model would need.

1.  Decoding them

   The mask is the one described in formats/bpt.txt: a byte-wise XOR
   against the Park-Miller generator seeded with 0x1a2b3c4d.

   The stream starts at byte 0 for these files, not after a leading
   count. That differs from CONFIG.BPT, which only decodes when the
   stream starts at byte 4. Scoring both alignments over a sample of
   .bpt files makes the split obvious: CARAC1 scores 1.00 from offset 0
   and 0.08 from offset 4, while CONFIG scores 0.96 from offset 4 and
   0.12 from offset 0.

   Why the two differ is not established. CONFIG.BPT and LIST.BPT are
   the two files the game chmods and rewrites at run time, so a
   plausible guess is that the runtime writer emits a plain header word
   while the build-time compiler does not [guess]. Do not rely on it;
   test both alignments.

   Every other .bpt sampled scores about 0.1 at both alignments, because
   those files contain strings and the stream desynchronises at the
   first one. CARAC files contain no strings, which is why they decode
   end to end.

   python tools/bpt.py --dump decomp/DATA/extracted/SPEED/CARAC1.BPT

2.  Layout

   73 values. Index 0 is an integer; treat the rest as floats.

   +----------+-----------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | Index    | Value in SPEED/CARAC1 | Reading                                    |
   +==========+=======================+============================================+
   | 0        | 2                     | version or record count                    |
   +----------+-----------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | 1 to 4   | 75, 75, 75, 75        | the four bike-select stat bars; see below  |
   +----------+-----------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | 5 to 27  | assorted              | body and handling constants, see below     |
   +----------+-----------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | 28       | 6                     | number of gears in use                     |
   +----------+-----------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | 29       | 12000                 | rev limit                                  |
   +----------+-----------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | 30 to 35 | 0.3 .. 1.45           | gear ratio array, six slots                |
   +----------+-----------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | 36       | 10500                 | a second, lower rev figure                 |
   +----------+-----------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | 37 to 71 | assorted              | grouped in threes, see below               |
   +----------+-----------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | 72       | 0                     | terminator                                 |
   +----------+-----------------------+--------------------------------------------+

2.1.  The gearbox is the anchor

   Index 28 gives the gear count and indices 30 to 35 hold the ratios.
   The array is fixed at six slots and index 28 says how many are live
   [verified]:

   +----------+-------+------------------------------+
   | Bike     | Gears | Slots 1 to 6                 |
   +==========+=======+==============================+
   | SPEED    | 6     | 0.3 0.6 0.9 1.2 1.34 1.45    |
   +----------+-------+------------------------------+
   | KTMCROSS | 5     | 0.35 0.65 0.95 1.1 1.3 (1.5) |
   +----------+-------+------------------------------+
   | SPEEDPKT | 4     | 0.24 0.44 0.64 1.0 (0.6 1.1) |
   +----------+-------+------------------------------+
   | KTMPKT   | 4     | 0.2 0.4 0.7 1.4 (1.0 2.0)    |
   +----------+-------+------------------------------+

   The two pocket bikes are what settle it. Their ratios climb cleanly
   for exactly four slots and then break the ramp, so the trailing slots
   are leftover rather than part of the sequence. Values in brackets are
   those unused slots.

   This also confirms the gearbox is real rather than decorative, which
   matches the Higher Gear and Lower Gear actions catalogued in
   subsystems/input-bindings.txt.

   Index 29 is above index 36 in every file, and both are ordered by
   bike class: 12000 and 10500 for the road bike, down to 9000 and 7000
   for the KTM pocket bike. A rev limit and a power or shift point
   [inferred].

2.2.  Values that look interpretable

   From SPEED/CARAC1, with the caveat that these readings are [guess]
   unless marked otherwise:

   [ 7] 280      plausibly top speed
   [12] 0.1963   very close to pi/16, so an angle in radians
   [20] 0.0028   small coefficient, drag or rolling resistance
   [24] -10      paired with
   [25] 10       a symmetric range, plausibly a lean or steering limit
   [26] -0.65    paired with
   [27] -0.55    a second, narrower symmetric pair

   From index 37 to 71 the values fall into descending groups of three,
   for example 6, 5.5, 4 then 10, 9, 8 then 3, 2.5, 2. Groups of three
   recur throughout. The game has three difficulty settings and
   CurrentDifficulty is a stored setting, so per-difficulty tuning is
   the obvious reading [guess]. It is equally consistent with three
   surface types.

3.  What varies

   Comparing the eight CARAC files within one bike, 18 of the 73 values
   differ. Comparing the four bikes at CARAC1, 60 of 73 differ.

   So the eight files per bike are variants of one machine rather than
   eight different machines: most of the table is shared and only the
   tuning moves.

   RESOLVED - THE EIGHT VARIANTS ARE THE EIGHT SELECTABLE BIKES.
   MenuPage10_BikeSelect indexes g_bikeCarac with 0..7 and refuses to
   step past either end, and the bike manifest names exactly eight
   carac scripts under the key "MotoCarac" [verified]. The gear
   ratios differing between them is then expected rather than
   suggestive: they are eight different machines.

   INDICES 1 TO 4 ARE THE FOUR STAT BARS page 10 draws -
   acceleration, speed, grip and braking - and they are NOT
   constant.  Only CARAC1 is 75/75/75/75, and it is that in every
   bank.  The other seven vary.  All four banks, stock:

   Note the type column. This note previously recorded that index 0 is
   an integer and the rest are floats. That is nearly right: indices 0
   to 4 are integers and so is index 72, PersoAnim. The remaining 67 are
   floats [verified].

   The ten triples are read as one named key followed by two bare
   Script_ReadFloat calls, so the file stores three numbers under each
   of those names. Nine of the ten are surfaces - neutral, deep water,
   water, sand, grass, earth, tarmac, oil and roadside - which makes
   that block a per-surface grip table. The tenth, MinAdherenceDerap, is
   the slide threshold and is stored the same way.

   Two values are adjusted after reading, which is worth knowing before
   comparing a file against memory [verified]:

       MotoType == 1     clamps RoueAccelerationAngulaire to
                         the range 3.0 to 6.0
       DAT_004dc1f4 == 0 subtracts a constant from NbSpeed

   IDENTIFIED. DAT_004dc1f4 is g_protectionPassed, the copy-protection
   verdict, and the constant is 1.0 - one gear fewer on every bike when
   the registry PId check fails. It is a silent anti-tamper penalty, not
   a parameter of the bpt format, and should not be reconstructed as
   one. This binary is cracked so the flag is always 1 and the penalty
   never applies. See subsystems/copy-protection.txt [verified].

   The vocabulary is French and mechanical throughout: Frein is brake,
   MotoMasse mass, Fourche the fork, RapportDeBoite a gearbox ratio,
   RegimeMax maximum engine speed, CoupleMax maximum torque, ZoneRouge
   the red line. So the model is a real engine and suspension
   simulation, not a set of arcade constants.

   Three fields are already known to be read elsewhere: 12, 13 and 14 as
   floats by maybe_Game_CollideRacers; 18, RealSpeedMax, copied to
   racer+0xb4 by Racer_Create at 0x0045c572; and 72, PersoAnim, used as
   a boolean by maybe_Game_LoadBikeMeshes and as the three-way lean and
   wheelbase gate in maybe_Racer_UpdateLean. See subsystems/racers.txt
   sections 4, 5 and 9.71.


5.  Open questions

   o Confirm whether the groups of three from index 37 are per-
     difficulty by locating a consumer indexed by CurrentDifficulty.
   o Index 12 being close to pi/16 is suggestive but unconfirmed; check
     whether the code treats it as an angle.
   o The reading of individual physics constants needs a consumer traced
     in the handling code before any of it can be trusted. Right now the
     gearbox is the only part backed by structural evidence.
