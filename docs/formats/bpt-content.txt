MotoRacer PC Reverse Engineering                           Analysis Note


                      WHAT THE .BPT FILES CONTAIN


Abstract

   Every .bpt in DATA now decodes end to end. This note records what
   each one holds. The format itself is in formats/bpt.txt and the bike
   characteristic tables, which live inside the banks rather than in
   DATA, are in formats/bpt-carac.txt.

   All fifteen files consume to exactly their last byte under
   tools/bpt.py --records, which is the check that the parse is right: a
   wrong string decision desynchronises the mask stream and the file no
   longer ends cleanly [verified].

2.  The track list

   LIST.BPT opens with a tag string, two zeroes and a count, then one
   record per track: display name, data directory, an ON or OFF flag,
   and one integer [verified].

   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+
   | Name        | Directory   | Flag | Value |
   +=============+=============+======+=======+
   | SPEED_BAY   | data\ile01\ | ON   | 4     |
   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+
   | SNOW_RIDE   | data\ile05\ | ON   | 2     |
   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+
   | ROCK_FOREST | data\ile03\ | OFF  | 4     |
   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+
   | LOST_RUINS  | data\ile04\ | OFF  | 2     |
   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+
   | WEST_WAY    | data\ile02\ | ON   | 4     |
   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+
   | GREAT_WALL  | data\ile06\ | OFF  | 3     |
   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+
   | RED_CITY    | data\ile08\ | OFF  | 3     |
   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+
   | DIRT_ARENA  | data\ile09\ | ON   | 2     |
   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+

   The tag is "PLUGIN". Four tracks are ON and four are OFF, and the
   trailing integer takes the values 2, 3 and 4. Neither field's meaning
   is established. MotoRacer splits its tracks between a road bike and a
   motocross bike, so the flag plausibly selects the category, but
   nothing read so far confirms it [guess].

   LIST01.BPT is the data disk's list. Its tag is "TWO_NEW_ISLANDS" and
   it swaps two tracks out for two new ones [verified]:

   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+
   | Name        | Directory   | Flag | Value |
   +=============+=============+======+=======+
   | FUN_FAIR    | data\ile10\ | ON   | 4     |
   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+
   | SEA_OF_SAND | data\ile11\ | ON   | 2     |
   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+
   | SPEED_BAY   | data\ile01\ | ON   | 4     |
   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+
   | SNOW_RIDE   | data\ile05\ | ON   | 2     |
   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+
   | WEST_WAY    | data\ile02\ | ON   | 4     |
   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+
   | GREAT_WALL  | data\ile06\ | OFF  | 3     |
   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+
   | RED_CITY    | data\ile08\ | OFF  | 3     |
   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+
   | DIRT_ARENA  | data\ile09\ | ON   | 2     |
   +-------------+-------------+------+-------+

   So the add-on does not extend the roster to ten. It keeps eight
   entries and drops ROCK_FOREST and LOST_RUINS to make room for
   FUN_FAIR and SEA_OF_SAND. The island directories confirm the naming:
   ile01 through ile09 for the originals, ile10 and ile11 for the
   additions, with ile07 unused.

3.  The championships

   A championship file is a count followed by that many race records.
   Each record is a track name, four integers, and a second track name
   that is usually NONE [verified].

   +--------+-------+---------------------+
   | File   | Races | Tracks used         |
   +========+=======+=====================+
   | CHAMPA | 12    | 6, each raced twice |
   +--------+-------+---------------------+
   | CHAMPB | 32    | 8                   |
   +--------+-------+---------------------+
   | CHAMPC | 32    | 8                   |
   +--------+-------+---------------------+

   CHAMPA runs SPEED_BAY, SNOW_RIDE, WEST_WAY, DIRT_ARENA, ROCK_FOREST
   and LOST_RUINS, then repeats the same six. The first of the four
   integers is 0 through the first pass and 1 through the second, so it
   reads as the round number [inferred]. CHAMPB and CHAMPC add RED_CITY
   and GREAT_WALL and run to 32 races.

   The 01 variants are the data-disk sets and follow their own track
   list: they use FUN_FAIR and SEA_OF_SAND and drop ROCK_FOREST and
   LOST_RUINS, matching LIST01.BPT exactly.

   The second name is NONE in almost every record. Where it is not, it
   repeats the name of the following record's track, which suggests a
   link between consecutive races rather than a property of one race
   [guess].

4.  The bike manifests

   The four bike files are almost entirely strings: an ordered manifest
   of every asset the bike needs [verified]. MOTOSPD.BPT, for the Speed
   bike, lists 58 and they fall into groups:

   +------------------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | Group                        | Examples                                   |
   +==============================+============================================+
   | texture banks                | bnk00_00.raw, bnk00_01.raw                 |
   +------------------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | skeleton                     | corps.skl                                  |
   +------------------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | wheels                       | rouear.3df and .map, roueav.3df and .map   |
   +------------------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | body, three levels of detail | medium\corps, kawalow, kawavlow            |
   +------------------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | cockpit                      | guidon, cockpit                            |
   +------------------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | riding animations            | static, gauche, droite, saut, derap*       |
   +------------------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | crash and finish             | chuteDos, fin2, fin3, fin7, fin8, finautre |
   +------------------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | podium animations            | data\podium\a1 through f1                  |
   +------------------------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | characteristics              | MotoCarac then carac1.spt to carac8.spt    |
   +------------------------------+--------------------------------------------+

   That last group is the join to formats/bpt-carac.txt. Each bike names
   eight carac files, the eight per-bike characteristic tables of 73
   values each, and they live inside the bike's bank rather than in
   DATA. Four bikes times eight tables is the 32 CARAC files already
   documented [verified].

   MOTOKTM.BPT carries twelve more entries than MOTOSPD.BPT, all of them
   stunt animations named sautdelire, and several are prefixed with _1_,
   _2_ or _3_. The prefix is on the string itself, not a separate field,
   so the loader must strip it. The pocket-bike files reuse the full-
   size bikes' animation paths in places, for example PKTSPEED referring
   to data\speed\ for its finish animations.

   A _<DIGIT>_ ENTRY IS FOLLOWED BY TWO VALUES, and a reader that does
   not consume them desynchronises the stream. maybe_Bike_ReadManifest
   calls FUN_00425080 twice for exactly those entries. "___name" is NOT
   one of them - isdigit('_') is false - so it carries no values, and
   MOTOKTM has both forms:

       _1_data\ktmcross\sautdelire.rot     800   0
       _3_data\ktmcross\sautdelire5c.rot  1200   0
       ___data\ktmcross\sautdelire5b.rot    (none)

   [verified]

   +----------+---------+---------+--------------+--------------+
   | manifest | strings | records | marker after | values to    |
   |          |         |         | n strings    | skip         |
   +==========+=========+=========+==============+==============+
   | MOTOSPD  |      58 |      58 |           49 |            0 |
   +----------+---------+---------+--------------+--------------+
   | PKTSPEED |      -  |      -  |           49 |            0 |
   +----------+---------+---------+--------------+--------------+
   | PKTKTM   |      -  |      -  |           54 |            6 |
   +----------+---------+---------+--------------+--------------+
   | MOTOKTM  |      72 |      90 |           63 |           18 |
   +----------+---------+---------+--------------+--------------+

   THE STREET MANIFESTS HAVE NONE, which is why a sequential reader
   looks correct until a KTM track is loaded. The original never
   notices: it finds MotoCarac by KEY with Settings_ReadString and
   rewinds the file first, so the stunt list cannot mislead it. Any
   port that scans for the marker instead must handle this.

5.  Saved configuration

   CONFIG.BPT is a player name followed by about fifty settings. The two
   copies in DATA differ in ways that read as real preferences: a sample
   rate of 11025 against 22050, and three volume values of 32 against 19
   [verified]. One stores a five-character player name and the other
   DSIZZZ.

   This resolves a question left open in formats/bpt.txt. The leading
   value of CONFIG.BPT was recorded there as a plain count of 5 that
   matched nothing in the file. It is not a count: it is the length
   prefix of the player name that follows. The same applies to the
   leading 23 in
   MOTOSPD.BPT, which is the length of data\speed\bnk00_00.raw
   [verified].

5a. Pocket mode is a second bike manifest

   LEVEL.BPT NAMES TWO, and a reader that takes only the first can
   never load a pocket bike:

       record 7   FileMoto, keyed     data\motoSpd.spt
       record 8   unkeyed follow-on   data\PktSpeed.spt

   ILE01 gives motoSpd/PktSpeed, ILE05 gives motoKTM/pktKTM, so all
   four of the manifests in DATA are reachable this way. There is no
   FilePocket key - record 8 trails FileMoto exactly as the .map
   paths trail each mesh key.  [verified]

   THE CHOICE IS ONE GLOBAL. Game_LoadBikeAndBanks at 0x0040a5bb:

       MOV EAX,[g_nPocketBikeSelected]   0x004dc078
       TEST EAX,EAX
       JZ  push 0x51d068                 record 7, full size
       PUSH 0x51d0a8                     record 8, pocket

   read live as 1 with pocket selected in the menu.  [verified]

   SO POCKET IS NOT A BIKE VARIANT. It is a different manifest
   naming different models, banks, .rot animations and carac files -
   which is why a port that ignores it shows the full-size bike no
   matter what the menu says.


6.  Open questions

   o The ON/OFF flag and the trailing 2, 3 or 4 in the track list are
     unexplained. Reading MenuPage23_DataDisk_Tick or
     MenuPage06_RaceSelect_Tick, which parse these files, would settle
     both.

   o Three of the four integers in a championship race record are 0 in
     every file. Only the first varies, and only between 0 and 1.

   o The second track name in a race record is NONE in almost every
     case. What the exceptions mean is unknown.

   o RESOLVED. maybe_Bike_ReadManifest at 0x00429e20 strips them while
     reading the PilotRot list: an entry starting '_' followed by a
     digit has the digit stored as a separate index in a parallel table,
     and the path is taken from character 3 onward. See subsystems/bike-
     assets.txt section 1.

   o ANSWERED. All 51 records are named, from the call order in
     Settings_LoadAll. See subsystems/config-file.txt.
