MotoRacer PC Reverse Engineering                           Analysis Note


                            OBJECT SOUNDS


Abstract

   Every track may ship a SOUND.BPT beside its geometry, and it is
   a small sound-design script rather than a list of cues.  It names
   samples, gives them fade envelopes, LFOs on volume and pitch,
   random pitch and pan, repeat counts and a distance falloff - and
   then attaches the result to named decor objects, so a gull or a
   corsair carries its own sound through the world.

   Six functions in the 0x00404xxx-0x00406xxx block, out of the
   sound half of animDecor.cpp.  Reconstructed in src/objsnd.c.

1.  The six functions

   +------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | Address    | What it does                               |
   +============+============================================+
   | 0x0042e700 | opens Sound.Spt through the script layer   |
   +------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | 0x0042e850 | the parser - soundDesc / ambiance / signal |
   +------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | 0x004056c0 | name -> cue id, interning as it goes       |
   +------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | 0x004059a0 | loads the samples the descriptors named    |
   +------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | 0x00405790 | binds a descriptor to every decor instance |
   +------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | 0x00405bc0 | the per-frame placement                    |
   +------------+--------------------------------------------+
   | 0x00405030 | the per-voice state machine                |
   +------------+--------------------------------------------+

   THE SCRIPT IS "Sound.Spt" AND THE FILE IS SOUND.BPT.  The loader
   rewrites any .spt request to .bpt - the same rule that turns
   Data\Config.Spt into DATA\CONFIG.BPT - so the shipped member is
   ILE01\SOUND.BPT and it reads with the ordinary bpt primitives.
   See formats/bpt.txt.  [verified]

2.  The script

   Three commands.  A .bpt HAS NO FRAMING, so every value has to be
   read whether or not it is used: one skipped field desynchronises
   the whole rest of the file and the parser has no way back in.

      soundDesc <id> <n> <name>*n <flags> <13 ints> <2 floats>
      ambiance  <n> <descId>*n
      signal    <signalKind> <n> <descId>*n

   THE FIRST NUMBER AFTER signal IS NOT AN ID, it is the signal
   kind, and the n values after it are descriptor ids - one
   entry registered per value.  Reading it the other way round
   is the obvious mistake and it parses cleanly either way.

   THE FLAG WORD is pipe-separated, e.g.
   "LOOPED|FADEIN|FADEOUT|FADEFREQ" - ten __stricmp tests in a chain
   at 0x0042eb00 onward.  [verified]

   +-------+----------+---------------------------------------+
   | Bit   | Name     | Effect                                |
   +=======+==========+=======================================+
   | 0x001 | LOOPED   | the voice loops rather than one-shots |
   +-------+----------+---------------------------------------+
   | 0x002 | PAN      | pan by direction from the camera      |
   +-------+----------+---------------------------------------+
   | 0x004 | RANDPAN  | a fresh random pan at each trigger    |
   +-------+----------+---------------------------------------+
   | 0x008 | VOL      | attenuate by distance                 |
   +-------+----------+---------------------------------------+
   | 0x010 | FADEIN   | ramp up over fadeInMs                 |
   +-------+----------+---------------------------------------+
   | 0x020 | FADEOUT  | ramp down over fadeOutMs              |
   +-------+----------+---------------------------------------+
   | 0x040 | FADEFREQ | ramp pitch across a fade              |
   +-------+----------+---------------------------------------+
   | 0x080 | INFPLAY  | never leave sustain                   |
   +-------+----------+---------------------------------------+
   | 0x100 | SINVOL   | sine LFO on volume                    |
   +-------+----------+---------------------------------------+
   | 0x200 | SINFREQ  | sine LFO on pitch                     |
   +-------+----------+---------------------------------------+

   PAN AND VOL ARE OPTED INTO SEPARATELY and that is the whole
   reason some things on a track follow the camera and others do
   not.  ressac has VOL without PAN: surf gets quieter with
   distance but stays centred, because it is not a point source.

3.  Cue ids are assigned at load

   The parser interns each sample name as it meets it and hands
   back index + 0x5d [verified, 0x00405716].  So OBJECT-SOUND IDS
   ARE NOT CONSTANTS - they count up from 0x5d per track and differ
   between tracks.

   That is why no object sample has a hardcoded id anywhere in the
   cue table, and why searching MOTO.EXE for "ressac" finds
   nothing: the names live in the encrypted .bpt, not in .rdata.

   The samples come from the Divers bank.  ILE01 interns 22 names
   across 18 descriptors, taking 0x5d..0x72.  [verified - live,
   both builds]

4.  The descriptor

   19 dwords, the array at 0x00502930 with a stride of 0x4c
   [verified, the copy loop at 0x0042ebbe].  Capacity 32; the
   parser throws past that.

   The field ORDER is the script's own - the parser reads them in
   sequence and the file stores no names.

   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | Offset | Field         | Type  | Notes                           |
   +========+===============+=======+=================================+
   | +0x00  | id            | int   | matched against the decor       |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x04  | firstSound    | int   | cue id of the first sample      |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x08  | sampleCount   | int   | how many; one picked at random  |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x0c  | flags         | uint  | section 2                       |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x10  | waitBase      | int   | ms of silence before a trigger  |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x14  | waitRand      | int   |                                 |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x18  | sustainBase   | int   | ALSO the LFO period - see 5.2   |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x1c  | sustainRand   | int   |                                 |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x20  | fadeInMs      | int   |                                 |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x24  | fadeOutMs     | int   |                                 |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x28  | repeatBase    | int   | plays before going quiet        |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x2c  | repeatRand    | int   |                                 |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x30  | freqBase      | int   | < 1 means the sample's own rate |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x34  | freqRand      | int   |                                 |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x38  | fadeFreqDelta | int   | pitch swing over a fade or LFO  |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x3c  | volume        | int   | 0..255                          |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x40  | pan           | int   | the fixed pan, -64..+64         |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x44  | radiusIn      | float | full volume inside this, metres |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+
   | +0x48  | radiusOut     | float | silent beyond this, metres      |
   +--------+---------------+-------+---------------------------------+

   EVERY "Rand" FIELD IS A SPREAD, NOT A MAXIMUM.  The slot takes
   base + Rand_Next(4) % rand, so 0 means "always exactly base"
   [verified, 0x00404ee0].  Every spread is drawn ONCE PER PLAY,
   which is what stops six gulls on one descriptor calling in
   unison.

   A freqBase UNDER 1 MEANS "THE SAMPLE'S OWN RATE" and the value
   is then an offset from it.  mouet asks for -1500, so the gulls
   call a little below the recording.

5.  The slot and its state machine

5.1.  The slot

   One playing sound.  Only the fields whose OFFSET matters are
   listed; the rest are in src/objsnd.h.

   +--------+----------+-------+-----------------------------+
   | Offset | Field    | Type  | Notes                       |
   +========+==========+=======+=============================+
   | +0x04  | voice    | int   | -1 when not playing         |
   +--------+----------+-------+-----------------------------+
   | +0x08  | sound    | int   | the sample THIS play drew   |
   +--------+----------+-------+-----------------------------+
   | +0x2c  | state    | int   | 5.2                         |
   +--------+----------+-------+-----------------------------+
   | +0x30  | cutoff   | float | radiusOut * 128             |
   +--------+----------+-------+-----------------------------+
   | +0x34  | slope    | float | 255 / ((rOut - rIn) * 128)  |
   +--------+----------+-------+-----------------------------+
   | +0x38  | lastDist | float | last frame's distance       |
   +--------+----------+-------+-----------------------------+
   | +0x3c  | stopped  | int   | the voice has been released |
   +--------+----------+-------+-----------------------------+

   THE THREE AT +0x30 ARE FLOATS AND THAT IS NOT A DETAIL.  The
   decompiler types the slot as int* and so renders the stores with
   an (int) cast, but the reads are FCOM [ESI+0x30], FMUL
   [ESI+0x34] and a float store to [ESI+0x38] - all three are
   floats.  [verified, 0x00405118 / 0x0040516f / 0x0040515d]

   SLOPE IS ALWAYS LESS THAN ONE.  For the ILE01 gulls it is
   255 / ((100 - 30) * 128) = 0.028, so believing the cast and
   holding it in an int truncates it to zero, and then every VOL
   sound on the track plays at volume nothing.  Silently: the voice
   still starts, the state machine still runs, and the log still
   reports the right number of bound objects.

   The 128 and the 255 are doubles at 0x004d0140 and 0x004d0148.
   128 is the world-units-per-metre scale the radii are given in.

5.2.  The states

   ORIGIN  FUN_00405030 @ 0x00405030.

       0 wait -> 1 start -> 2 fadeIn -> 5 sustain -> 4 fadeOut
                     |                                   |
                     +------ (no FADEIN) --> 3 stop <-----+

   State 3 decrements the repeat count and re-arms at state 0 if
   any are left, so repeatBase is plays-per-burst rather than a
   lifetime.  INFPLAY pins the slot in 5 for ever and neither the
   sustain timer nor the repeat count is consulted again.

   THE TWO LFOs SHARE sustainBase AS THEIR PERIOD.  The original
   reads descriptor +0x18 for both, which is the same field the
   sustain LENGTH comes from - one value doing two jobs, not a
   transcription slip.  vent asks for 22000 ms, so the wind
   breathes once every twenty-two seconds; four copies of it drawn
   from different spreads never line up.

   SINVOL swings the volume about its base by +/-100 per cent, and
   SINFREQ swings the pitch by +/-fadeFreqDelta - the same field
   FADEFREQ ramps, again doing two jobs.

   RANDPAN IS DRAWN AT THE TRIGGER, not per frame: the birds
   scatter around you and then hold still while each call plays
   out.  A per-frame draw would sound like static.

6.  The distance model and the pan

   THE DISTANCE MODEL IS THE SECOND GATE, NOT THE FIRST.  An
   object sound is only considered at all when its segment is
   within 60 segments of the player's, wrapping
   (Trk_IsSegmentNearPlayer, 0x0040d350, called from
   ObjSnd_TickAll at 0x00405bf9 with 0x3c).  Kind 0 is also
   skipped outright when the detail level is 0 - skipped, not
   ticked with audible 0, so a sounding voice is left alone
   rather than released.

   THAT WINDOW IS DOING MORE WORK THAN IT LOOKS.  radiusOut is
   often big enough to cover most of a track, so without it a
   prop is audible from anywhere - ile01's aeroplane is bound to
   segment 378 of 788 and reaches the grid at segment 3.  On the
   grid, eight of that track's eleven object sounds are inside
   the window and three are not.  [verified - live, both
   builds]

   Within it, distance is measured from the CAMERA EYE, not from
   the bike - the same eye maybe_Snd_TickRace uses for the
   racers, so a gull and a bike never disagree about which is
   nearer.

       vol = clamp((cutoff - dist) * slope, 0, 255)

   THE DESCRIPTOR'S OWN volume IS NOT A FACTOR HERE.  The distance
   result REPLACES it rather than scaling it [verified, 0x00405168
   - nothing reads +0x3c between the FMUL and the clamp].

   CROSSING OUT OF RANGE RELEASES THE VOICE, which is what keeps a
   small voice pool enough for a track with forty sounding objects.
   It takes TWO consecutive frames outside to do it - the test is
   dist > cutoff AND lastDist > cutoff - so a single frame of
   camera snap does not cut a sound off.  [verified, 0x00405126]

   PAN IS THE SINE OF THE ANGLE OFF THE CAMERA'S FORWARD: the 2D
   cross of the flattened forward with the normalised direction to
   the object, scaled to the script's -64..+64.  Same expression as
   race_audio_pan uses for the bikes, and with NO dead zone - a
   gull is a point that moves past you, and the jitter the racers'
   dead zone exists to suppress does not arise.

7.  Placement

   FUN_00405bc0 has three arms, and the difference between them is
   audible.

   +------------+-----------------------------------+---------------+
   | Kind       | Where the sound is                | Reconstructed |
   +============+===================================+===============+
   | AT_OBJECT  | the decor instance's own position | yes           |
   +------------+-----------------------------------+---------------+
   | AT_SEGMENT | a track segment's origin          | no - see 8    |
   +------------+-----------------------------------+---------------+
   | AT_GLOBAL  | nowhere; always audible           | no - see 8    |
   +------------+-----------------------------------+---------------+

7.2.  The emitter is a point ON the model, not its origin

   ObjSnd_TickAll's kind-0 arm rotates an EMITTER OFFSET by the
   instance's euler triple and adds the instance position
   [0x00405cd0 onward].  The offset is `*(instance+0x4c) + 4` -
   the model's vertex array - and the point used is its FIRST
   VERTEX.  Confirmed to the digit: MOTO's corsair3 reads
   (17585.61, 332.32, 878.44) and so does the port's
   named[23].mesh[0].verts[0].  [verified - live, both builds]

   FOR ANYTHING THAT TURNS THIS IS THE WHOLE EFFECT, not a
   refinement.  ile01's aeroplane NEVER TRANSLATES - a write
   watchpoint on its instance position caught nothing across a
   whole flypast - and only its three euler angles move.  The
   model origin is the PIVOT of the circular flight path and the
   aircraft sits 17610 units, 137 metres, out on that arm.
   Rotating the instance is what flies it.

   Read the sound off the pivot and it never moves: constant
   distance, constant volume, a drone instead of a pass that
   swells and fades.  MOTO's distance ran 4156 -> 476 -> 6802
   over 64 frames where the port sat at 16203 throughout.

   USE THE DECOR EULER (FUN_00403880), the one trk_draw places
   props with - the sound must come from where the model is
   DRAWN.  mat34_from_euler is a different matrix and tilts the
   emitter off anything carrying pitch or roll.

7.3.  Placement kinds

   A gull moves with its model; a wind anchored to a segment does
   not; an arena rumble has no position at all and is neither
   panned nor attenuated.

7.1.  How a signal finds its segment

   ambiance calls ObjSnd_AddAmbiance(descId) and registers kind 2.
   signal calls ObjSnd_AddSignal(descId, signalKind) and registers
   kind 1, keeping signalKind at header +0x08.  The kind is
   REJECTED unless 5 < kind < 13, so the seven legal values are
   6..12 and they map to BITS 0..6 of a per-segment flag word.

   EVERY SEGMENT CARRIES THAT WORD AT +0x0f8, AND IT IS NOT READ
   FROM THE FILE.  Trk_Load computes it in a pass of its own at
   0x0040e0da, immediately after AnimDecor_Load.  [verified]

   THE ENCODING IS A TOGGLE AND THAT IS THE WHOLE TRICK.  The
   segment field `extra` (+0x0ec) carries a signal kind on the
   segments where a span STARTS or ENDS, and -1 - anything
   outside 6..12 - everywhere else.  Walking the track in order
   and XORing bit (kind - 6) at each marker leaves every segment
   holding the set of spans it lies inside:

       acc = 0
       for each segment in order:
           if 6 <= extra <= 12:  acc ^= 1 << (extra - 6)
           segment.signalFlags = acc

   So two markers bound a REGION rather than naming a point.
   ile01 opens kind 8 at segment 0, and every segment along the
   water carries bit 2 from there on - segment 2 reads extra -1
   and signalFlags 0x04.  READING THE MARKERS AS POINTS instead
   would put the surf at one post and is the obvious wrong
   answer.  [verified - live]

   The accumulator is not reset per lap, so an unclosed span
   simply runs to the end of the track.

   ObjSnd_TickAll rebuilds a SEVEN-ENTRY TABLE every frame, one
   slot per bit, by calling Trk_FindSignalSegments
   (FUN_0040d3b0) with a window of 40.  That scans the 80
   segments from cur-40 to cur+40, wrapping, and for each bit
   keeps the segment carrying it whose origin is NEAREST the
   current segment's origin - squared distance, no line of
   sight, Y included.  A bit no segment in the window carries
   leaves -1, and a kind-1 entry resolving to -1 is simply not
   audible that frame.

   So a signal sound follows the nearest piece of the track that
   is TAGGED with its kind, and goes quiet once that tag is more
   than forty segments away.  It is how surf stays by the water
   without being attached to any object.

   Read live on ILE01 with the player at the grid, the table at
   0x005041ec held segment 34 for kind 6, segment 2 for kind 8,
   and -1 for the other five.  ILE01's four signal entries are
   kinds 8, 8, 9 and 9, so two of them were sounding and two
   were dormant.  [verified - live]

   THE SEARCH WINDOW IS 40, NOT THE 60 SECTION 6 USES.  Two
   different literals in the same function - 0x28 at 0x00405bab
   for the span search and 0x3c at 0x00405bf9 for the decor
   audibility test.

   THE ENTRIES ARE APPENDED DURING THE PARSE, before the samples
   are loaded, and the decor objects are appended after - which
   is the order MOTO's list reads back in.  A slot armed that
   early cannot ask a sample for its rate, so anything with
   freqBase < 1 has to be re-armed once the samples exist.

   BINDING WALKS THE PER-SEGMENT DECOR LISTS, not the flat
   instance array, and the segment it finds each object under is
   kept - that segment is what section 6's window tests.  A flat
   walk finds the same objects and cannot say where they are.
   Each segment's list is its indexCount/indices pair, which the
   original keeps at +0xd4 and +0xb4.

   An instance listed by two segments binds twice, as it does in
   the original - two slots drawing their spreads separately.  No
   shipped track does it.

   BINDING IS BY THE DECOR INSTANCE'S soundDesc FIELD, matched
   against descriptor +0x00.  The default is -1 and NOT 0, because
   0 is a valid descriptor id - ILE01's gulls are descriptor 0, so
   a calloc'd instance would otherwise bind every unsounded object
   on the track to the seagulls.  Capacity 40; the original raises
   a message box past it.

8.  Open questions

   o WHAT THE SEVEN SIGNAL KINDS MEAN is not known.  The
     mechanism is fully reconstructed and the spans are read
     off the track, but nothing says which kind is water,
     which is crowd and so on - only that ile01 uses 6, 8 and
     9.  Naming them wants a second track to compare against.

   o WHICH MESH THE EMITTER COMES FROM.  The offset is the first
     vertex of the object's model (7.2), and we always read mesh
     0 where the original follows whichever LOD the instance
     currently points at.  corsair3's three LODs share a first
     vertex so nothing separates them; a template whose LODs
     disagreed would move its emitter as the detail changed.

   o WHAT DECIDES audible IN THE PLACEMENT is only partly read.
     Segment distance round the track and the detail level are both
     involved; the exact test is not transcribed.

   o THE SAMPLE NAMES ARE NOT CROSS-CHECKED against the Divers
     bank.  A descriptor naming a sample that bank does not hold
     logs and is skipped, which would hide an off-by-one in the
     interning as a handful of missing sounds.
