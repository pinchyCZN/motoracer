MotoRacer PC Reverse Engineering                           Analysis Note


                        THE HUD COUNTER


Abstract

   The instrument cluster in the lower right of a race: a small rev
   counter with the gear number inside it, a larger speedometer with
   the digital speed inside it, two needles and their drop shadows.

   The game calls it the COUNTER, and two settings drive it -
   ShowCounter and CounterType.  Hud_DrawCounter (0x00484810) is the
   whole of it except the dial FACES, which are meshes and belong to
   the track.

1.  The settings

   +------------------+------------+-------------------------------+
   | Setting          | Global     | Meaning                       |
   +==================+============+===============================+
   | ShowCounter      | 0x004f0dc8 | 0 draws nothing at all        |
   +------------------+------------+-------------------------------+
   | CounterType      | 0x004f0dcc | 0 off, 1 dials + digits,      |
   |                  |            | 2 digits only                 |
   +------------------+------------+-------------------------------+
   | SpeedUnit        |            | 1 = mph                       |
   +------------------+------------+-------------------------------+
   | SmallFonts       | 0x004f0e08 | with ScreenMode, halves it    |
   +------------------+------------+-------------------------------+

   Both counter settings are CONFIG.BPT items 23 and 24 and both
   default to 1.  NEITHER HAS A ROW ON THE OPTIONS PAGE: one in-race
   key cycles the whole cluster, at 0x00432107 [verified]:

       if (!ShowCounter)      turn it on
       else if (!CounterType) turn it on
       else if (type == 1)    Hud_CycleCounterType(2)
       else                   Hud_CycleCounterType(0)

   so the order is off -> dials -> digits -> off.  They are the
   game's settings even though the options screen never shows them.

   THE SIZE SWITCH IS ONE BOOLEAN used everywhere below:

       hi = (SmallFonts == 0 && ScreenMode == 2)

   so the full-size layout is 640x480 with big fonts and everything
   else gets the half-size one.

   MPH IS INTEGER AND EXACT [verified]:

       v = v * 0x3e800 / 0x649 >> 8      i.e. v * 1000 / 1609

2.  Where it sits

   One origin, at 0x00484d60, and it is the only thing in the
   function tied to the frame [verified]:

       hi    (g_viewWidth - 195, g_viewTopMargin + g_viewHeight - 140)
       else  (g_viewWidth - 102, g_viewTopMargin + g_viewHeight -  74)

   kept in g_hudCounterOriginX / Y (0x005dbc80 / 0x005dbc84).
   Everything in section 3 is an offset from it, so the cluster
   hugs the bottom-right corner at any frame size.

3.  The layout table

   Recomputed only when CounterType, ScreenMode or SmallFonts
   changes.  Offsets from the origin, before the type-1 shift
   [verified]:

   +-----------------+------------------+------------------+
   | Item            | full size        | half size        |
   +=================+==================+==================+
   | speed digits    | (0xcf, 0xd2)     | (0xe2, 0x4f)     |
   +-----------------+------------------+------------------+
   | gear digit      | (0x43, 0x6a)     | (0x1f, 0x33)     |
   +-----------------+------------------+------------------+
   | speedo hub      | (0xcd, 0xbf)     | (0xe3, 0x44)     |
   +-----------------+------------------+------------------+
   | speedo zero     | (0xbb, 0xfa)     | (0xdb, 0x63)     |
   +-----------------+------------------+------------------+
   | speedo full     | (0xf2, 0x8e)     | (0xf9, 0x2a)     |
   +-----------------+------------------+------------------+
   | tacho hub       | (0x77, 0xd3)     | (0xb7, 0x4f)     |
   +-----------------+------------------+------------------+
   | tacho zero      | (0x6c, 0xf7)     | (0xb1, 0x63)     |
   +-----------------+------------------+------------------+
   | tacho full      | (0x77, 0xaa)     | (0xb7, 0x39)     |
   +-----------------+------------------+------------------+
   | shadow offset   | (-3, -3)         | (-2, -2)         |
   +-----------------+------------------+------------------+
   | type-1 shift    | (0x3d, 0x74)     | (0x9a, 0x1e)     |
   +-----------------+------------------+------------------+

   EVERY NUMBER ABOVE IS A RAW MOV IMMEDIATE, read at 0x004849df
   (full) and 0x00484a9b (half).  DO NOT take them from the
   decompiler: four of the slots are typed float, so the constants
   render as denormals like 3.06884e-43, and converting one of those
   by hand is how the half-size speedo zero came to be written down
   as 188 instead of 0xdb.

   WITH CounterType == 1 MOST OF THE CLUSTER MOVES by minus the
   shift, and only then are the dials built at all.  Type 2 keeps
   the unshifted positions and draws digits over bare track.

   THE GEAR TEXT IS NOT SHIFTED, AND THAT IS NOT AN OVERSIGHT.  The
   block at 0x00484b74 has SIX subtractions and no seventh: the
   speed text (0x005f14e8/ec) and the six dial points on the stack.
   0x005f1500/04 is never touched [verified].

   The effect is deliberate.  The tacho hub moves to (58, 95) while
   the gear digit stays at (67, 106) - just below and right of it,
   INSIDE the small dial.  Shifting the gear as well throws it 61
   pixels left and 116 up at full size, and 154 left in the
   half-size layout where the shift X is larger.

   The shift is uniform, so it moves the hubs and their two
   reference points together and THE ANGLES ARE UNCHANGED by it.

4.  The dial

4.1.  Hud_DialInit

   Hud_DialInit (0x00485be0) builds a dial from THREE SCREEN POINTS -
   the hub, where the needle sits at minValue, and where it sits at
   maxValue.  Nothing is authored as an angle [verified].

   +--------+------+--------+-------------------------------------+
   | Offset | Size | Type   | Field                               |
   +========+======+========+=====================================+
   | +0x00  |   4  | int    | cx                                  |
   +--------+------+--------+-------------------------------------+
   | +0x04  |   4  | int    | cy                                  |
   +--------+------+--------+-------------------------------------+
   | +0x08  |   4  | int    | minValue                            |
   +--------+------+--------+-------------------------------------+
   | +0x0c  |   4  | int    | maxValue                            |
   +--------+------+--------+-------------------------------------+
   | +0x10  |   8  | double | angleMin                            |
   +--------+------+--------+-------------------------------------+
   | +0x18  |   8  | double | angleMax                            |
   +--------+------+--------+-------------------------------------+
   | +0x20  |   8  | double | radPerUnit                          |
   +--------+------+--------+-------------------------------------+
   | +0x28  |   8  | double | radius                              |
   +--------+------+--------+-------------------------------------+

       radius   = |(zeroX, zeroY) - (cx, cy)|
       angleMin = atan2(cy - zeroY, zeroX - cx)
       angleMax = atan2(cy - fullY, fullX - cx)

   Y IS NEGATED IN BOTH atan2 CALLS, so the angles are in the
   ordinary maths convention even though screen Y grows downward.
   Section 5 flips it back.

   Both angles are then normalised into [0, 2pi), and ONE of them is
   pushed a full turn negative so the sweep runs the short way:

       reversed == 0   if (angleMin < angleMax) angleMax -= 2pi
       reversed != 0   if (angleMax < angleMin) angleMin -= 2pi

       radPerUnit = (angleMax - angleMin) / (maxValue - minValue)

   which comes out NEGATIVE for a clockwise gauge.  Both shipped
   dials pass reversed = 0.

   THE RADIUS COMES FROM THE ZERO POINT ONLY.  The full-scale point
   sets the angle and is not required to be the same distance out.

4.2.  The two shipped dials

   +----------+----------+--------+-----------+-------------------+
   | Dial     | hub      | radius | range     | angle at zero     |
   +==========+==========+========+===========+===================+
   | speedo   | 144, 75  |  61.7  | 0..300    | 253.0 deg         |
   | full     |          |        | km/h      |                   |
   +----------+----------+--------+-----------+-------------------+
   | tacho    |  58, 95  |  37.6  | 0..16000  | 253.0 deg         |
   | full     |          |        | rpm       |                   |
   +----------+----------+--------+-----------+-------------------+
   | speedo   |  73, 38  |  32.0  | 0..300    | 255.5 deg         |
   | half     |          |        | km/h      |                   |
   +----------+----------+--------+-----------+-------------------+
   | tacho    |  29, 49  |  20.9  | 0..16000  | 253.3 deg         |
   | half     |          |        | rpm       |                   |
   +----------+----------+--------+-----------+-------------------+

   Hubs are relative to the cluster origin, after the type-1 shift.
   The half-size rows were READ LIVE out of a paused MOTO at
   g_hudDialSpeedo and g_hudDialTacho and agree with the layout
   table to the bit [verified]:

     speedo  cx 0x49 cy 0x26 max 0x12c  angleMin 4.4599
             radPerUnit -0.011971  radius 32.0156
     tacho   cx 0x1d cy 0x31 max 0x3e80 angleMin 4.42092
             radPerUnit -1.78133e-4 radius 20.884, angleMax = pi/2

   BOTH NEEDLES REST WITHIN THREE DEGREES OF EACH OTHER, pointing
   down and slightly left - about 6:30 on a clock.  That is the
   check worth running after any change to the layout table: if the
   two diverge visibly at a standstill, a zero point is wrong.  It
   is what caught the 188.

   THE SWEEP RUNS CLOCKWISE ON SCREEN even though radPerUnit is
   negative, because the Y flip of 4.1 turns a decreasing maths
   angle into a clockwise one.  The speedo covers 200 degrees and
   the tacho 163.

   THE SPEEDO READS TO 300 KM/H and no bike reaches half of it, so
   the needle lives in the first third of the sweep.  That is the
   original's scale, not a mistake.

5.  The needle

5.1.  The four points

   The needle is four points in DIAL-LOCAL POLAR FORM.  Each is an
   (along, across) pair, placed by [verified, 0x00485284 onward]:

       a = clamp(value, minValue, maxValue) * radPerUnit + angleMin
       x = cx + along*cos(a) - across*sin(a)
       y = cy - along*sin(a) - across*cos(a)

   with each term truncated to int by __ftol before it is added, and
   the cluster origin added afterwards.

   +----+--------+---------------+---------------+
   | v  | role   | along         | across        |
   +====+========+===============+===============+
   | 0  | back   | -5 * (1 + hi) | 0             |
   +----+--------+---------------+---------------+
   | 1  | tip    | (int) radius  | 0             |
   +----+--------+---------------+---------------+
   | 2  | left   | 0             | -3 * (1 + hi) |
   +----+--------+---------------+---------------+
   | 3  | right  | 0             | 3 * hi + 3    |
   +----+--------+---------------+---------------+

   so at full size the needle is a kite 10 px behind the hub, out to
   the rim, 6 px either side.  Two triangles, (0,1,2) and (0,3,1), in
   two shades so it reads as lit down one edge:

       Color_Pack16(0x19, 0x10, 0)   the (0,1,2) half
       Color_Pack16(0x1f, 0x10, 0)   the (0,3,1) half

   THE TACHO NEEDLE USES THE BRIGHT COLOUR FOR BOTH, so only the
   speedo needle is two-toned.

5.2.  The shadow

   The same four points minus the shadow offset - so DOWN AND RIGHT
   by 3 at full size - flat black, queued at camZ 8 against the
   needle's 4.  The queue walks back to front, so 8 lands behind.

5.3.  The vertex fields

   MR_Vertex, 0x1c, screen-space already [verified]:

       +0x08 camZ    2048.0 with no z-buffer, 0.0 with one -
                     EXCEPT the shadow vertices, which carry 1.0
                     either way
       +0x0c shade   0x3e000, i.e. intensity 31, full bright
       +0x10 screenX
       +0x14 screenY
       +0x18 invZ    1/2048 or 1.0, matching camZ's branch

   The camZ values do not order anything - the queue depth does - so
   they only reach the rasterizer's perspective term.

6.  The values

   All three come from the VIEWED racer, not the local one
   [verified]:

       speed  g_racers[viewed] + 0x118   the measured speedometer
       gear   g_racers[viewed] + 0x7c
       tacho  g_racers[viewed] + 0x11c   FILD at 0x00485844

   The speedometer is the ground-truth number Racer_UpdateDash
   measures off the rear tyre, not engineSpeed - see
   racers.txt 9.48.0.6.

   The gear digit is written as a CHARACTER, and which one depends on
   the type:

       type 1    gear + '7'      ':', ';', '<' ...
       type 2    gear + '0'      '0', '1', '2' ...

   so type 1 indexes a glyph set further up the font rather than the
   ASCII digits.

6.0.  THE TACHO VALUE IS NORMALISED TO THE REDLINE

   Hud_DrawCounter does NOT feed racer+0x11c to the dial. It rescales
   it first [verified, 0x00485844]:

       shown = racer+0x11c / RegimeMax * 16000

   FILD racer+0x11c, FDIV g_bikeCarac+0x74 - that offset is field 29,
   RegimeMax - then FMUL the dword at 0x005f14bc, which holds 0x3e80
   = 16000 and is the dial's own full scale.

   THE TWO NUMBERS ARE NOT THE SAME SCALE, which is the whole point.
   racer+0x11c is real rpm and tops out at RegimeMax - 12000 on the
   stock bike - while the dial sweeps 0..16000 (4.2). So the redline
   is mapped onto FULL SWEEP, and a bike with a different RegimeMax
   still uses the whole dial.

   o SKIPPING IT PARKS THE NEEDLE AT THREE QUARTERS. 12000/16000, and
     the needle never enters the red band at all. The port did this
     until it was caught by eye - both games paused at 179 km/h in
     gear 3 held tachoRpm 10913 and 10907, but MOTO displayed 14550
     and the port 10907. 10913 / 12000 * 16000 = 14550.67.

   o AND THE DATA MATCHING IS WHY IT SURVIVED. Every field feeding
     the counter agreed to within rounding, so a field-by-field A/B
     said the tacho was correct. The difference was in a step that
     has no stored value - only the needle shows it.

6.1.  The tacho slew limit

   The tacho needle is limited to 3000 rpm of movement per frame
   against the last displayed value in 0x004f0e10 [verified]:

       d = tacho - lastShown
       if (d >  3000) d =  3000
       if (d < -3000) d = -3000
       shown = max(lastShown + d, 0)
       lastShown = shown

   WHICH IS WHAT STOPS IT SNAPPING ACROSS THE DIAL ON A GEAR CHANGE.
   The speedo needle has no such limit; Racer_UpdateDash already
   damps that one, and only for an AI.

6.2.  The two readout fonts are LCD art

   Hud_DrawCounter asks Font_Get for three ids, and FONTS.BPT names
   them [verified, MR_Font.txt 450]:

   +------+------------------+------+------------------------------+
   | Id   | Name             | Type | File                         |
   +======+==================+======+==============================+
   | 0x10 | SpeedLcdLowRes   | Pal  | FONTES\LOWRES\SPEEDLCD.FNT   |
   |  15  | SpeedLcdHiRes    | Pal  | FONTES\HIRES\SPEEDLCD.FNT    |
   +------+------------------+------+------------------------------+
   | 0x17 | MotorLcdLowRes   | Pal  | FONTES\LOWRES\MOTORLCD.FNT   |
   |  22  | MotorLcdHiRes    | Pal  | FONTES\HIRES\MOTORLCD.FNT    |
   +------+------------------+------+------------------------------+
   | 0x1c | CounterHiRes     | Pal  | FONTES\HIRES\SPEED.FNT       |
   |  29  | CounterLowRes    | Pal  | FONTES\LOWRES\SPEED.FNT      |
   +------+------------------+------+------------------------------+

   Font_Get swaps each id for its twin on SmallFonts, so the code
   asking for the LowRes member gets the HiRes one at 640x480.

   THEY ARE Pal FACES, so every colour is in the face's own 256-entry
   palette and none of it is set by Hud_DrawCounter.  A Pal face must
   NOT be given foreground and background colours; that is the rule
   GameMenu follows in the escape menu too.

   READ OUT OF THE FILES [verified]:

   +--------------+-------+-------+--------------------------------+
   | Face         | first | count | palette                        |
   +==============+=======+=======+================================+
   | SPEEDLCD.FNT | 0x20  |  26   | 0 key, 1..15 dark grey to      |
   |              |       |       | amber (198,148,8), 16 yellow   |
   +--------------+-------+-------+--------------------------------+
   | MOTORLCD.FNT | 0x30  |  14   | 0 key, 1..8 dark red to red    |
   |              |       |       | (255,0,0), 9..15 red to grey   |
   +--------------+-------+-------+--------------------------------+
   | SPEED.FNT    | 0x30  |  12   | 0 key, 1..7 white to black,    |
   |              |       |       | 8..15 olive to yellow          |
   +--------------+-------+-------+--------------------------------+

   INDEX 0 IS (0, 0, 255) IN ALL THREE and every glyph blitter skips
   pixel value 0, so it is the colour key.  The speed digits are
   AMBER and the gear digit is RED, in the art.

   o SO THE GREEN LCD PLATE IS NOT IN THE FONT.  It is painted into
     the dial face - the "counter" decor mesh of section 7 - and the
     digits are drawn over it.  Without that mesh the readouts are
     bare segments on the track, which is what the reconstruction
     shows today.

   o AND MOTORLCD STARTS AT 0x30 WITH FOURTEEN GLYPHS, which is what
     makes gear + '7' sensible: gears 0..6 index the second half of
     the face, 0x37 to 0x3d.

6.2.1.  The two SpeedLcd faces are INVERTED, and that is the art

   Rendered out of the files, glyph '1' [verified]:

       LOWRES  8 x 10   cell is index 2 (36,33,23), near black.
                        The DIGIT is drawn BRIGHT, indices 8..10.
       HIRES  16 x 18   cell is index 15 (198,148,8), bright amber.
                        The DIGIT is drawn DARK, indices 1..12.

   So the small face is a bright digit on a dark panel and the large
   one is a dark digit on a bright panel.  Neither is wrong; they are
   different art.

   THE CELL IS SLANTED.  Index 0 - the colour key - fills a
   parallelogram notch at the top left and the bottom right of every
   HiRes glyph, which is what gives the readout its italic lean.

   AND THE HiRes CELL HAS A DARK BAND ALONG ITS TOP.  Row 0 is index
   5 (74,59,19), rows 1 to 4 ramp 13, 14, 14, 14 and the panel proper
   starts at row 5 with index 15.  Three digits side by side
   therefore show a soft dark bar ABOVE them.

   o IT IS IN THE FILE, NOT IN THE BLITTER.  FontPal_DrawGlyph
     (0x00495840) is 898 bytes of which 113 differ from
     Font_DrawGlyph, and every one of those is register allocation -
     the inner loop is the same `if (b) *dst = colorLut[b]`.  There
     is no per-row handling to miss.

   o AND IT IS INVISIBLE AT LOWRES because that whole cell is dark
     already.  A band that only appears with large fonts on is the
     expected result of these two faces, not a porting defect.

6.2.2.  The gear digit sits differently on the plate in each mode

   The plate scales by (1.9, 2.0) while the gear text offset is a
   separate authored pair per mode, so the two do not track:

   +-------+-------------+--------------+---------------------+
   | Mode  | gear at     | plate        | fraction across it  |
   +=======+=============+==============+=====================+
   | full  | 512, 446    | 462..641,    | 0.279, 0.754        |
   |       |             | 345..479     |                     |
   +-------+-------------+--------------+---------------------+
   | half  | 569, 457    | 546..640,    | 0.245, 0.672        |
   |       |             | 412..479     |                     |
   +-------+-------------+--------------+---------------------+

   THAT DRIFT IS THE ORIGINAL'S.  Every number in it is a raw
   immediate, and MotorLcd's two faces are 21 and about 11 tall,
   which moves the apparent centre further again.  A reconstruction
   that matches the layout table will show it.

6.3.  Both readouts are left aligned

   Hud_DrawCounter passes flags 0x21 to Font_DrawTextInternal, which
   hands them to Font_ApplyAlignment (0x00492c20) [verified]:

       h = flags & 0x1f        v = flags & 0xe0

       h == 2 or 4       x = (g_viewWidth - textW) / 2
       h == 8 or 0x10    x = (g_viewWidth - x) - textW
       otherwise         x unchanged

       v == 0x40         y = (g_viewHeight - textH) / 2
       v == 0x80         y = (g_viewHeight - y) - textH
       otherwise         y unchanged

   0x21 gives h = 1 and v = 0x20, and neither matches any case, so
   BOTH COORDINATES PASS THROUGH UNTOUCHED.  The function's own
   guard proves that is deliberate rather than a gap in the ladder:

       if ((flags & 0x1f) != 1 || (flags & 0xe0) != 0x20)
           Font_MeasureText(...)

   0x21 is the one combination that skips the measure, because it is
   the one that needs no width.

   o THE FLAG IS NOT WHERE THE JUSTIFICATION LIVES, THOUGH.  The
     format string at 0x004f10e0 is "%3d", not "%d" - read out of a
     running MOTO - and SPEEDLCD's SPACE GLYPH IS 16 PIXELS WIDE,
     the same as every digit.  So printf pads the number to three
     characters, the padding renders as blank LCD cells, and the
     readout grows LEFTWARD out of a fixed three-cell field.

     That is why the face covers 0x20..0x39: 0x21 to 0x2f are
     one-pixel filler and the only glyphs that matter are the space
     and the ten digits.

     So "right justified" is true of what you SEE and false of the
     alignment flag.  Both halves matter: h == 8 or 0x10 is the flag
     that would do it, and nothing in the counter asks for it.

   o Font_ApplyAlignment IS NOT Text_ApplyAlignment (0x00417900).
     Two alignment schemes with different bit layouts: this one
     serves every Font_DrawText caller, that one serves the menu
     widgets and the in-race menu.

   o AND THE COUNTER'S COORDINATES ARE NEVER HALVED.
     Font_DrawTextInternal halves x and y when its first argument is
     0 and the mode is small; Hud_DrawCounter goes through the
     wrapper at 0x00493fe0, which passes 1.  The half-size layout
     table is the only halving there is.

7.  The dial face is a track decor quad

   THE CIRCLES, TICK MARKS, RED BAND, BLACK PIE AND THE GREEN LCD
   PLATE BEHIND THE SPEED READOUT ARE ALL ONE TEXTURED QUAD.  It is
   drawn by Hud_DrawSignMeshes (0x00486310) at queue depth 15 -
   behind both needles - and Hud_DrawCounter draws none of it.

   Read live out of a paused MOTO [verified]:

       record  0x005d2eb8   mesh index 14, anchor (93, 0),
                            +0x0c active = (CounterType == 1),
                            +0x10 cleared
       mesh    4 vertices, 2 faces, name "counter" at +0x70
       verts   (0,0) (94,0) (94,67) (0,67), z within +-1.8
       faces   ONE QUAD WOUND BOTH WAYS - vertexIndex 2,1,0,3 and
               3,0,1,2, wrap slot set so both are quads
       uv      texels (14,178) to (107,245) over a 256 page

   THE TWO FACES ARE MIRRORED, NOT IDENTICAL.  Their UVs turn with
   the winding: face 0 maps v2 to u 0.418 and face 1 maps v3 to it.
   The original queues BOTH and lets the rasterizer's winding test
   throw one away, which is the point of the pair - whichever way the
   quad faces, exactly one of them draws.

   o A PORT THAT QUEUES BOTH DRAWS TWO GAUGES MIRRORED ABOUT A
     VERTICAL AXIS.  Ours did: cull_and_queue_faces is where the
     winding test lives here, and queueing a primitive directly goes
     round it.  See section 8.

   AND IT SHIPS INSIDE EVERY .trk.  The string "counter" appears in
   all eleven track files and nowhere else in the data except
   DESIGN.BKF's directory entry for COUNTER\TRANSP.RAW.  So the
   plate is an ORDINARY DECOR TEMPLATE, reached by name exactly the
   way maybe_Trk_CountCameras reaches the CAMERAs - which also means
   maybe_Hud_BindCornerSigns cannot run before a track is loaded, and
   it does not: maybe_Game_InitRace (0x0047bf70) is its only caller.

7.1.  The placement

   The anchor is an INSET FROM THE BOTTOM RIGHT, not a position
   [verified, 0x0048650c onward]:

       offsetX = g_viewMaxX - anchorX * scaleX
       offsetY = g_viewMaxY - anchorY * scaleY
       screenX = vx * scaleX + offsetX
       screenY = offsetY - vy * scaleY

   so the mesh's +Y runs UP the screen, and (93, 0) with scale 1 puts
   the plate at x 546..640, y 412..479 in a 640x480 frame.

   THE PLATE IS AUTHORED AT THE HALF-SIZE LAYOUT [verified,
   0x00486464]:

       screenMode == 2 && !smallFonts    scale 1.9, 2.0
       otherwise                         scale 1.0, 1.0

   which is the other half of why the cluster is bigger with big
   fonts on: 94 x 1.9 is 179 and 67 x 2.0 is 134, against the
   full-size layout's 195 x 140 footprint.

7.2.  The six corner signs share the mechanism

   Slots 1 to 6 of the same 16-entry table hold g_45, d_45, g_90,
   d_90, g_chica and d_chica - gauche and droite, 45 and 90 degree
   corners and a chicane - all anchored at (320, 400), which the
   formula above puts near the TOP of the frame.  They blink on a
   timer counted down with g_frameDeltaScaled and are gated on
   DisplaySignal.  Not reconstructed.

8.  The port

   src/hud.c.  The dials, needles, shadows, digits and the face are
   all reconstructed.

   ONE DIVERGENCE IN THE FACE, and it is about WHERE the winding
   test happens rather than whether.  The original relies on its
   rasterizer to drop the back-facing half of the plate; our back end
   does not cull, so hud_counter_draw_face applies cull.c's own cross
   product - same expression, same 1.0f - before queueing. Routing
   through cull_and_queue_faces instead would work but would take the
   queue depth from camZ and lose the original's 15.

   THE NEEDLES GO THROUGH THE QUEUE, not the framebuffer, because
   that is where the original puts them.  Drawing them as 2D
   triangles would put them under the track.

   ONE FORCED DIVERGENCE: THE VERTICES ARE BACK-PROJECTED.  The
   original submits SCREEN coordinates and uses camZ only as the 1/z
   its texture mapper wants.  The port's scene path works in CAMERA
   space - drawscene.c's fill_vertex reads camX/camY/camZ and lets
   the device matrix project them, so that D3D9 can clip properly -
   so a screen pixel has to be turned back into one:

       camX = (px - g_viewCentreX) * z
       camY = (g_viewCentreY - py) * z

   inverting transform.c's own formula.  z is g_nearPlaneZ * 1.25,
   just past the near plane, which puts the needles in front of
   everything in the track; the queue depth still orders needle
   against shadow.

   THE OVERLAY HAS ITS OWN TEXTURE.  Sharing one with the 2D layer
   put opaque leftovers immediately outside the rect a composite
   uploads, and every sample that reached them - a linear tap, or
   the driver rounding the dirty rect outward - drew a faint
   rectangle around the readouts.  Point sampling removed three
   sides and left the bottom, which is the tell that it was an
   UPLOAD boundary and not only a filter tap.  A second texture,
   cleared to alpha 0 once, has nothing to bleed from.

   o WRITING camX = camY = 0 AND TRUSTING screenX DRAWS NOTHING.
     Every vertex collapses onto the projection centre and the four
     triangles come out degenerate - no error, no warning, an empty
     corner of the screen.  That is how this first landed.

   THE OVERLAY RECT IS MEASURED, NOT THE WHOLE CORNER.  It is the
   union of the two text boxes plus a few pixels.  A rect that ran
   from the cluster origin to the bottom-right of the frame was
   wrong twice: it sheared the top off the gear digit, and its
   composite quad sampled stale opaque texels out of the shared 2D
   layer texture, drawing a faint rectangle around the corner.

9.  Open questions

   o THE TYPE-2 GEAR HINT BAR.  Under CounterType 2 the function
     builds a run of repeated characters whose length is
     g_mainWidth/8 and draws it at g_mainHeight/16 with a colour
     chosen from a five-way shift-state ladder.  Read but not
     transcribed; it is a shift-up prompt, not part of the cluster.

   o WHETHER CounterType 3 EXISTS.  The layout branch is
     "type == 2 ... else if (type != 0)", so a third value would get
     the dial layout with the dials never initialised.  Nothing in
     CONFIG.BPT or the menu produces one.

   o THE HALF-HEIGHT BRANCH at the end of each needle block halves
     every Y when 0x00624b14 is set and the hardware path is off.
     An interlaced or half-vertical mode; not identified.
