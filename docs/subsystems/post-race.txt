MotoRacer PC Reverse Engineering                           Analysis Note


                      THE POST-RACE SEQUENCE


Abstract

   Crossing the line does not end a race - it starts a timer.  Seven
   seconds normally, fourteen when the winner's bike is to keep
   riding itself while the credits roll, and only then does the game
   go back to the menus.

   The same machinery serves three things, which is why it reads as
   one subsystem rather than three: the end of a race, the
   title-screen attract demo, and replay playback.  All three switch
   on the SAME trackside shot director, through one condition in
   Camera_SelectView.

   This note covers the end-of-race half.  The director itself is in
   camera.txt 5; the credits are not ported.

1.  The sequence

   FUN_00412a50 (0x00412a50) runs every frame from maybe_Game_Tick
   (0x0047d9f9).  Its whole state is one millisecond countdown,
   DAT_004e9824, stepped by the integer frame delta - with two
   values that are not durations:

   +-------+----------------------------------------------------+
   | Value | Meaning                                            |
   +=======+====================================================+
   |   -1  | idle - waiting for the race to end                 |
   +-------+----------------------------------------------------+
   |  > 1  | counting down                                      |
   +-------+----------------------------------------------------+
   |   1   | expired; the transition is due                     |
   +-------+----------------------------------------------------+
   |   0   | done; dispatch back to the menus                   |
   +-------+----------------------------------------------------+

   From idle, g_nRaceOverHud or g_nRaceOverNet going non-zero arms
   it at 7000 ms and plays a cue.  If the auto-ride gate then says
   yes it is re-armed at 14000 instead - the extra seven seconds are
   the credits' window, not a longer pause.

   At 1 the transition runs: start the credits once, and when they
   report finished, put the player back and fall to 0.  At 0 the
   dispatch differs by mode - championship, single race and practice
   each go somewhere different, and time attack writes its record on
   the way.

2.  The auto-ride, and who gets it

   FUN_00412960 (0x00412960) is the gate, and it is four tests:

       not a replay
       solo
       g_nSelectedMode == 1          SINGLE RACE only
       finishPosition == 0           FIRST PLACE only

   SO THE AUTO-RIDE IS FOR A WINNER.  Second place onward simply
   stops at the line, which is what a fourth-place finish shows -
   no camera cuts and no riding on.  Practice and championship never
   get it at all.

   THE HANDOVER IS ONE FIELD.  The tick dispatch already forks on
   racer +0x08, humanPlayer, so clearing it hands the bike to the
   AI.  Everything else is making that not look like a glitch:

   +----------------+---------+-------------------------------+
   | Field          | Offset  | Action                        |
   +================+=========+===============================+
   | humanPlayer    | +0x08   | 0 - the handover              |
   +----------------+---------+-------------------------------+
   | engineSpeed    | +0xec   | = speed                       |
   +----------------+---------+-------------------------------+
   | aiRoadSpeed    | +0x1a4  | = speed                       |
   +----------------+---------+-------------------------------+
   | realSpeedMax   | +0xb4   | *= 0.8  (0x004d03a8)          |
   +----------------+---------+-------------------------------+
   | tickInputWord  | +0x2dc8 | 0                             |
   +----------------+---------+-------------------------------+

   THE ORDER MATTERS in one place: the two speeds are seeded BEFORE
   the ceiling is scaled, so the AI inherits the speed actually
   being carried rather than the reduced maximum.  Scaling first
   would brake the bike on the frame of the handover.

   It also clears the whole input record - both axes to centre
   (0x8000), every button off, Input_Clear 0x00470fc0 - because a
   key still held at the line would otherwise fight the AI for as
   long as it was down.  The counter is hidden and the cockpit view
   forced off.  The restore reverses all of it.

3.  What the port does

   race.c carries the sequence, the gate and the director;
   racer.c the per-racer handover; trk.c the camera search.  No new
   file: each piece went where rule 13 already puts that kind of
   code.

   +---------------------------+---------------------------------+
   | race_post_race_tick       | FUN_00412a50                    |
   +---------------------------+---------------------------------+
   | race_post_race_reset      | FUN_00410120, its sequence half |
   +---------------------------+---------------------------------+
   | race_auto_ride_gate       | FUN_00412960                    |
   +---------------------------+---------------------------------+
   | race_auto_ride            | DAT_00504ea0, for the director  |
   +---------------------------+---------------------------------+
   | camera_update_trackside   | Camera_UpdateTrackside          |
   +---------------------------+---------------------------------+
   | racer_ai_takeover         | the field table above           |
   +---------------------------+---------------------------------+
   | racer_restore_human       | the transition's restore        |
   +---------------------------+---------------------------------+
   | trk_find_nearest_camera   | Trk_FindNearestCamera           |
   +---------------------------+---------------------------------+

   mr_place.py --attract declares the bike home in first and lets
   the sequence arm, so testing needs no lap.  It is single-race
   only for the same reason the gate is.

   THREE DELIBERATE DIVERGENCES:

   o THE CREDITS ARE A STUB.  race_credits_begin logs and reports
     "finished", so the fourteen-second window expires on its TIMER
     rather than on the credits ending.  Behaviourally close, not
     the same thing.

   o THE NETWORK ARM IS ABSENT.  With neither flag set the original
     still runs a 3000 ms countdown on DAT_0052c238, the "everybody
     else has finished" path, which a solo port cannot reach.

   o THE DIRECTOR IS REACHABLE ONLY THROUGH THE AUTO-RIDE.
     Camera_SelectView switches it on for any of five flags; the
     other four are the replay and the attract demo, and the port
     has neither yet.

4.  Open questions

   o The credits subsystem - FUN_0048acd0, FUN_0048ae40,
     FUN_0048a9e0, FUN_0048b070 and the element destructors.  A
     self-contained C++ presentation with its own object arrays and
     CD audio track 12.  Where it should live in the port is
     deliberately undecided until it is read.

   o FUN_00412a50's state 0 for CHAMPIONSHIP.  It goes to page
     0x18 or FUN_004580f0 and is not reached yet.  The solo
     single-race arm is done - it raises the post-race question,
     FUN_00437b70, [REPLAY] over [YES] and [NO] - and so is
     practice, which opens the continue prompt and, in time
     attack, writes the ghost on the way.  What practice still
     lacks is FUN_00464680, the bestscor.dat table that runs
     immediately before the prompt; see formats/timatak.txt 6.

   o ITALIAN AND SWEDISH FOR [REPLAY].  The .rdata groups the
     translations by concept, Sw..Us ascending, with the resource
     pointer on the Us one; four of the six were located and the
     other two fall back to English rather than being invented.

   o The gearbox re-seat FUN_0048df90, run last in the restore.  It
     lands one frame before the game leaves for the menus, so the
     gear the player nominally resumes in is not observable; its
     ratio table is reached by a NEGATIVE index off
     MinAdherenceDerap and was left rather than guessed.

   o DAT_00504eac IS CLOSED, and it was not the plaque's own flag.
     It means THE RESULT HAS BEEN ACKNOWLEDGED - by a key through
     Input_OnKeyDown, or by the sequence reaching its transition at
     0x00412b0c, beside the credits start.  The plaque flying out
     is one of the things that follows, which is why a port that
     never set it left the plaque up for ever.

   o FUN_00404da0(0x3e, 500), the post-race cue - a different entry
     point from audio_cue, and the 500 is unread.
