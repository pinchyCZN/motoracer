MotoRacer PC Reverse Engineering                           Analysis Note


                           D3D RENDER STATE


Abstract

   The hardware path changes render state in two places: a bulk block
   written once per execute buffer, and six small emitters called from
   inside the queue walk in D3D_DrawScene. The bulk block was already
   recorded in d3d-execute-buffers.txt section 2. This note covers the
   six, which were unnamed, undocumented, and are the half of the
   hardware seam that renderer.txt section 3.4 was missing.

   Between them they decide blending, texture filtering and dithering
   for every primitive the game draws, and they answer two questions
   this project had left open - what DAT_0053be0c selects, and what
   bit 3 of the primitive-kind feature table means.

   +-----------------------------+------------+
   | Item                        | Address    |
   +=============================+============+
   | D3D_SetBlendMode            | 0x0041dde0 |
   +-----------------------------+------------+
   | D3D_SetBlendModeFlatAlpha   | 0x0041e000 |
   +-----------------------------+------------+
   | D3D_SetBlendModeSrcAlpha    | 0x0041e1b0 |
   +-----------------------------+------------+
   | D3D_SetTextureFilter        | 0x0041e2b0 |
   +-----------------------------+------------+
   | D3D_SetDither               | 0x0041e380 |
   +-----------------------------+------------+
   | D3D_InvalidateBatch         | 0x0041e730 |
   +-----------------------------+------------+
   | D3D_EmitRenderStates        | 0x0041a0a0 |
   +-----------------------------+------------+
   | the consumer, D3D_DrawScene | 0x004af570 |
   +-----------------------------+------------+

1.  Two layers of state

   D3D_EmitRenderStates at 0x0041a0a0 runs once per execute buffer,
   from D3D_BeginExecuteBuffer, and installs twelve or thirteen states
   in one instruction - shade mode, Z enable, Z func, fill mode,
   specular, antialias and the rest. Those are the defaults for the
   whole buffer and they do not change while it is being filled
   [verified].

   The six emitters in this note are called from inside the bucket walk
   in D3D_DrawScene, once per primitive, and each appends a further
   D3DOP_STATERENDER ahead of the geometry it applies to. They are
   therefore the dynamic layer: everything that varies between one
   primitive and the next [verified].

   +------------+------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
   | Address    | Name and arguments                 | Render states it can emit                |
   +============+====================================+==========================================+
   | 0x0041dde0 | D3D_SetBlendMode(enable, useAlpha) | SRCBLEND DESTBLEND ALPHABLENDENABLE      |
   |            |                                    | STIPPLEDALPHA ALPHATESTENABLE            |
   |            |                                    | TEXTUREMAPBLEND                          |
   +------------+------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
   | 0x0041e000 | D3D_SetBlendModeFlatAlpha(enable)  | the same set, minus the PowerVR choice   |
   +------------+------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
   | 0x0041e1b0 | D3D_SetBlendModeSrcAlpha(enable)   | ALPHABLENDENABLE SRCBLEND DESTBLEND      |
   |            |                                    | ALPHATESTENABLE TEXTUREMAPBLEND          |
   +------------+------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
   | 0x0041e2b0 | D3D_SetTextureFilter(linear)       | TEXTUREMAG TEXTUREMIN                    |
   +------------+------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
   | 0x0041e380 | D3D_SetDither(enable, force)       | DITHERENABLE                             |
   +------------+------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
   | 0x0041e730 | D3D_InvalidateBatch()              | none - one store, see section 4.1        |
   +------------+------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+

   FIVE of the six are __thiscall on g_pCd3d (0x00624afc), and every
   call site in D3D_DrawScene loads ECX from that global before the
   call [verified]. An earlier revision of this note said all six; that
   was wrong. D3D_InvalidateBatch is __stdcall and takes no arguments -
   it writes one global and never touches the Cd3d object. Grouping it
   with the other five describes what it does, not how it is called.

   THE FIVE NOW CARRY MR_Cd3d * ON this. The signature pass
   had recorded that as impossible and section 8.6 closes it: a
   __thiscall auto-this cannot be retyped as a parameter, but it can be
   typed by putting the method in a GhidraClass whose name matches the
   structure. Their bodies now read

       this->pInstrCursor

   where they read *(int *)(param_1 + 8) before, so the cursor
   arithmetic in section 2 is legible directly in the decompiler rather
   than only in this note.

   Note the two sides live in different translation units. D3D_DrawScene
   at 0x004af570 is in the screen3d\screen3d.cpp region; all six
   emitters, and the geometry emitters they sit alongside, are in
   Dsi_d3d.cpp. So the hardware seam is a file boundary too, which is
   convenient for a port: Dsi_d3d.cpp is the file to replace, and
   screen3d.cpp is the caller that stays. See code-map.txt.


2.  The instruction, and where it is written

   Each emitter writes a D3DINSTRUCTION and its payload straight into
   the execute buffer through a cursor held in the Cd3d object, and
   advances it. Two cursors are now confirmed [verified]:

   +-----------+-----------------------------------------------------+
   | Field     | Use                                                 |
   +===========+=====================================================+
   | Cd3d+0x04 | vertex write cursor, stepped 0x20 per D3DTLVERTEX   |
   +-----------+-----------------------------------------------------+
   | Cd3d+0x08 | instruction write cursor, stepped 4 for the header  |
   |           | then 8 per D3DSTATE                                 |
   +-----------+-----------------------------------------------------+

   The encoding is the one already recorded in d3d-execute-buffers.txt
   section 2 - BYTE bOpcode = 8 D3DOP_STATERENDER, BYTE bSize = 8,
   WORD wCount, then wCount records of { DWORD stateType, DWORD value }.
   The emitters compute wCount up front and write it before the
   payload rather than patching it afterwards, which is why the count
   appears as a literal 3, 4, 5 or 6 in the disassembly [verified].

2.1.  Four render states not previously catalogued

   The table in d3d-execute-buffers.txt section 2 was drawn from
   D3D_EmitRenderStates alone. These four appear only in the dynamic
   layer and should be read as additions to it [verified]:

   +------+-----+------------------------------------------------+
   | Hex  | Dec | D3DRENDERSTATE_                                |
   +======+=====+================================================+
   | 0x11 | 17  | TEXTUREMAG                                     |
   +------+-----+------------------------------------------------+
   | 0x12 | 18  | TEXTUREMIN                                     |
   +------+-----+------------------------------------------------+
   | 0x13 | 19  | SRCBLEND                                       |
   +------+-----+------------------------------------------------+
   | 0x14 | 20  | DESTBLEND                                      |
   +------+-----+------------------------------------------------+

   With those, every state number the binary emits decodes against
   D3DRENDERSTATETYPE, and every value against D3DBLEND,
   D3DTEXTUREFILTER, D3DTEXTUREBLEND, D3DSHADEMODE or D3DCMP. Nothing
   in the stream is a private encoding.


3.  Blend mode is chosen by primitive kind

   The selection is a flat ladder on the primitive kind word at +0x3a,
   at 0x004afaac to 0x004afb65 [verified]:

   +---------------------+------------------------------+-----------------------------+
   | Kind                | Call                         | Resulting SRC / DEST        |
   +=====================+==============================+=============================+
   | 0x44, 0x45          | D3D_SetBlendModeFlatAlpha(1) | INVSRCALPHA / INVSRCALPHA   |
   +---------------------+------------------------------+-----------------------------+
   | 0x0c                | D3D_SetBlendMode(1, 0)       | ONE / SRCCOLOR              |
   +---------------------+------------------------------+-----------------------------+
   | 0x2c, 0x3e          | D3D_SetBlendMode(1, 1)       | INVSRCALPHA / INVSRCALPHA,  |
   |                     |                              | or SRCALPHA / INVSRCALPHA   |
   |                     |                              | on PowerVR                  |
   +---------------------+------------------------------+-----------------------------+
   | textured, and the   | D3D_SetBlendModeSrcAlpha(1)  | SRCALPHA / INVSRCALPHA      |
   | bank carries alpha  |                              |                             |
   +---------------------+------------------------------+-----------------------------+
   | everything else,    | D3D_SetBlendMode(0, 0)       | blending off                |
   | including 0 and 5   |                              |                             |
   +---------------------+------------------------------+-----------------------------+

   The textured-with-alpha row is the only conditional one. It is
   taken when -Alpha (DAT_004dc1c0) or DAT_004dc1bc is set, AND the
   bank record flags word has both bit 0x10 and bit 0x02, AND the
   primitive is textured. Otherwise that case falls through to
   blending off [verified].

   "TEXTURED" HERE IS THE STATE MASK'S BIT 0, which D3D_DrawScene
   builds FROM THE KIND ALONE - set for anything that is not kind 0, 5
   or 0x0c - without asking whether a texture is actually bound
   [verified]. That is NOT the same test as the colour-route selector
   of structs/MR_Vertex.txt section 6.5, which additionally requires a
   bound texture slot. The two overlap and a primitive can satisfy one
   and not the other.

   THOSE TWO BITS NOW HAVE MEANINGS. texture-banks.txt section 5.2
   decodes MR_Bank.flags from the two functions that set it:

       0x02  the texture source is 16-bit
       0x10  a colour key was applied

   So the condition reads "a colour-KEYED 16-BIT texture", which is
   exactly the case that needs real per-texel alpha rather than the
   half blend of section 3.1 - a keyed texture has genuinely
   transparent texels, and blending them at a flat 50 percent would
   show the key colour through. The rule is not arbitrary.

   The address given in an earlier draft as 0x004f954c is the flags
   field of BANK 0. The bank array base is 0x004f92f8 and flags is at
   +0x254; that draft treated 0x004f9300 as the base, which made every
   offset 8 bytes short. See texture-banks.txt section 1.

   So there are exactly three blends in the whole hardware renderer:
   half, add-modulate, and conventional source alpha. Everything else
   is opaque.

3.1.  The half blend, and why both factors are INVSRCALPHA

   SRCBLEND and DESTBLEND are BOTH set to 6 D3DBLEND_INVSRCALPHA on
   non-PowerVR hardware. That looks like a mistake and is not
   [verified].

   The alpha the primitive carries is 0x80 - see section 3.2 - so
   1 - srcAlpha is 0.5, and the blend evaluates to

       dst = 0.5 * src + 0.5 * dst

   a symmetric half blend. PowerVR gets SRCALPHA / INVSRCALPHA, the
   conventional pair, which with the same 0.5 produces the identical
   result. Two routes to one appearance, presumably because one of the
   two combinations was unsupported or wrong on some 1997 part
   [inferred - the code states the split, not the reason].

   o For a port this is worth reading twice. The naive translation
     "SRCBLEND was INVSRCALPHA, so set D3DBLEND_INVSRCALPHA" is
     faithful, but the INTENT is a 50 percent blend, and it only comes
     out at 50 percent because the alpha is hardcoded to 0x80. If a
     port ever lets that alpha vary, the non-PowerVR pair stops being
     equivalent to the PowerVR one and the two diverge.

   The odd one out is kind 0x0c, which takes ONE / SRCCOLOR -
   additive modulated by the source colour, a brightening blend rather
   than a transparency one.

3.2.  The alpha byte is stamped into the flat colour

   Immediately before the state selection, D3D_DrawScene forces the
   top byte of the primitive colour at DAT_00709370 [verified]:

       *(byte *)(prim + 0x3f) = 0xff;          /* the record own copy */
       if (kind == 0x2c || kind == 0x3e || kind == 0x0c ||
           (kind == 0x44 && g_devDoesAlphaFlatStippled))
           *(byte *)0x00709373 = 0x80;         /* alpha = 0x80 */

   0x00709373 is the high byte of DAT_00709370, so this sets the alpha
   of the flat colour to 0x80 for exactly the kinds that will be
   blended. That is where the 0.5 of section 3.1 comes from, and it is
   the reason the half blend works out.


4.  The four caches

   The dynamic layer would emit a state block per primitive if it were
   naive. It is not - each emitter holds a cached copy of what it last
   sent and skips the write when nothing changed [verified]:

   +------------+--------------------+---------------------------------------+
   | Global     | Caches             | Seeded by                             |
   +============+====================+=======================================+
   | 0x004e4f2c | blend on / off     | D3D_EmitRenderStates, to 0            |
   +------------+--------------------+---------------------------------------+
   | 0x004e4f34 | texture filter     | D3D_SetTextureFilter itself           |
   +------------+--------------------+---------------------------------------+
   | 0x004e4f38 | dither             | D3D_EmitRenderStates, to              |
   |            |                    | (DAT_004e4f14 == 1)                   |
   +------------+--------------------+---------------------------------------+
   | 0x004e4f3c | the open batch key | everything, to -100                   |
   +------------+--------------------+---------------------------------------+

   The first three are ordinary redundant-state elimination. The
   fourth is not a state at all.

4.1.  The batch key is the interesting one

   D3D_EmitTriangle does not emit one instruction per triangle. It
   opens a D3DOP_TRIANGLE header with wCount 0, remembers its address
   in DAT_0053bcf0, and increments that count in place for every
   subsequent triangle - so a run sharing one texture costs one
   header. What closes a batch is the key it is handed differing from
   DAT_004e4f3c.

   D3D_InvalidateBatch writes -100 into that key, a value no real key
   takes, which forces the next triangle or line to re-emit
   TEXTUREHANDLE and start a fresh header. Every state emitter ends by
   writing it inline, for the obvious reason: a state change that
   landed in the middle of an open batch would otherwise apply to
   triangles already counted in it [verified].

   D3D_DrawScene also calls it standalone at two points the emitters
   do not cover [verified]:

   o the texture slot bound for a bank changed - the cache for that is
     per-bank at 0x004f9620 + bank*0x338
   o the assembled state mask changed, where the mask is bit 0
     textured, bit 3 blended, bit 4 has a texture slot

   That mask is also what D3D_EmitTriangle tests bit 0 of to decide
   whether to emit a real texture handle or zero.

   o For a port: this is a batching optimisation, not semantics. A
     D3D9 back end that sets state per draw call and never batches
     produces identical output, more slowly. The one thing to preserve
     is the ORDER - state before the geometry it applies to - which
     falls out naturally from any immediate-mode translation.


5.  Texture filtering is bit 3 of the feature table

   D3D_SetTextureFilter emits TEXTUREMAG and TEXTUREMIN together, both
   set to 2 D3DFILTER_LINEAR or 1 D3DFILTER_NEAREST. There is no
   mipmapping - the values 3 to 6 of D3DTEXTUREFILTER, which are the
   MIP ones, are never written anywhere in the state stream
   [verified].

   It has two callers, and each answers a question [verified]:

   o D3D_EmitRenderStates passes DAT_0053be0c directly, once per
     buffer. So DAT_0053be0c is the bilinear filtering switch.
     renderer-mode.txt recorded stored renderer mode 2 as "mode 0 with
     DAT_0053be0c = 1" and could not say what distinguished the two
     hardware variants. It is filtering: mode 0 is hardware point
     sampled, mode 2 is hardware bilinear. The global is written by
     Main_InitAndParseCommandLine and Settings_LoadAll, persisted by
     Settings_SaveAll, and toggled live by a key in Input_OnKeyDown at
     0x00431a91 / 0x00431a9f.

   o D3D_DrawScene passes ((&DAT_006d1f88)[kind] & 8) == 0, per
     primitive, and only when DAT_0053be0c is non-zero. So bit 3 of
     the primitive-kind feature table of renderer.txt section 3.5.3
     means "do not filter this kind". That is one of the eight bits
     decoded.

   Note the array is dwords - the access is [kind*4 + 0x6d1f88] - so
   the feature table is 0x46 dwords, not bytes.

   The function also declines to emit anything if the requested value
   already matches DAT_004e4f34, and forces the value to 0 when
   DAT_0053be0c is clear, so filtering off is sticky rather than
   re-asserted per primitive.


6.  Dithering

   D3D_SetDither emits DITHERENABLE alone, guarded by the cache at
   DAT_004e4f38 unless its second argument forces the write. Its
   configuration global is DAT_004e4f14, the -NoDither setting
   [verified].

   D3D_DrawScene skips the call entirely when DAT_004e4f14 is -1, and
   otherwise passes 0 in place of DAT_004e4f14 when the vertex flag
   bit 0x40000000 is set. So that vertex bit suppresses dithering for
   its primitive - a per-primitive override of a global setting
   [verified].

   The same bit is passed to D3D_EmitTriangle and D3D_EmitLine in the
   high byte of their first argument, so it is read twice for two
   purposes.

   RESOLVED. The bit means FULL BRIGHT. D3D_WriteVertex reads the same
   vertex word and, when bit 30 is set, writes a white diffuse colour
   instead of looking up a shade - see structs/MR_Vertex.txt section
   6.2 [verified]. Suppressing dither for it is consistent: an
   unshaded white vertex has no gradient to dither.

   The word at vertex +0x0c is packed - an 8-bit intensity at bits
   10..17, bit 30 full bright, bit 31 "the low 24 bits are a literal
   colour". That section has the whole layout.


7.  What a D3D9 back end does with this

   Every state in the dynamic layer has a direct D3D9 equivalent, and
   nothing here is execute-buffer specific except the encoding
   [verified as to the source side]:

   +--------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
   | Existing state           | D3D9                                           |
   +==========================+================================================+
   | SRCBLEND / DESTBLEND     | D3DRS_SRCBLEND / D3DRS_DESTBLEND, same enum    |
   |                          | values                                         |
   +--------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
   | ALPHABLENDENABLE         | D3DRS_ALPHABLENDENABLE                         |
   +--------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
   | ALPHATESTENABLE          | D3DRS_ALPHATESTENABLE - always 0 here          |
   +--------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
   | DITHERENABLE             | D3DRS_DITHERENABLE                             |
   +--------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
   | TEXTUREMAG / TEXTUREMIN  | SetSamplerState MAGFILTER / MINFILTER          |
   +--------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
   | TEXTUREMAPBLEND          | no equivalent - see below                      |
   +--------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
   | STIPPLEDALPHA            | dropped - no equivalent, and it is a fallback  |
   +--------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
   | TEXTUREHANDLE            | SetTexture                                     |
   +--------------------------+------------------------------------------------+

   Two need a decision rather than a translation:

   o TEXTUREMAPBLEND was removed after DirectX 6 in favour of texture
     stage states. The binary only ever sets it to 2 MODULATE or 1
     DECAL, chosen once in D3D_EnumDeviceCallback from the device
     dwTextureBlendCaps bit D3DPTBLENDCAPS_MODULATE - modulate if
     supported, decal otherwise. On any modern part modulate is
     supported, so a port hardcodes the modulate stage setup:
     COLOROP = MODULATE, COLORARG1 = TEXTURE, COLORARG2 = DIFFUSE
     [verified as to what the binary chooses].

   o STIPPLEDALPHA is only emitted when the device lacks
     DoesAlphaFlatBlend, i.e. it is the 1997 fallback for hardware
     that could not blend a flat alpha and faked it with a stipple
     pattern. A modern back end always has real blending, so this
     whole branch - and the wCount 6 and 4 variants that carry it -
     can be dropped. Dropping it is what makes the emitters simpler in
     a port, not more complex.

   ALPHABLENDENABLE takes DAT_004e4f10, which Cd3d_Ctor sets to the
   constant 1 and nothing ever changes, so it reads as a literal TRUE
   [verified].


8.  Open questions

   o RESOLVED. All eight bits of the feature table at DAT_006d1f88 are
     decoded in detail-levels.txt - it is the level-of-detail system.
     Bit 3 is corroborated there by a second consumer, FUN_004232e0 at
     0x00423393, which computes DoesFilter && !(bits & 8) using the
     device capability that d3d-textures.txt section 8.11 names from
     the game's own diagnostic format strings.
   o What sets the vertex flag bit 0x40000000 that suppresses
     dithering, and what it marks geometrically.
   o THREE OF THE FIVE ARE NOW IDENTIFIED, from their software span
     fillers [verified]:
         0x0c  untextured translucent polygon - dst/2 + flatColour/2
         0x2c  colour-keyed textured, 50/50 blended, AFFINE
         0x3e  the same, PERSPECTIVE-CORRECT
     They are the three kinds carrying feature bit 4, which
     detail-levels.txt section 2.6 establishes as translucency, and
     0x2c and 0x3e differ by exactly bit 5 - the perspective bit. So
     the hardware half blend of section 3.1 and the software half
     blend are one operation seen from two back ends.
   o 0x44 and 0x45 are still unidentified, but the search has been
     narrowed considerably and NO PRODUCER WAS FOUND. Four methods
     came up empty [verified]:

       1  no immediate store of 0x44 or 0x45 to memory exists anywhere
          in the image - a scan of all 239,429 instructions for
          MOV [mem], 0x44 and MOV [mem], 0x45 returns nothing
       2  the four face constructors (FUN_00422be0, FUN_00422c80,
          FUN_00422d20, FUN_00422ed0) are never called with either
          kind; the kinds their fifteen call sites pass are
          0, 1, 2, 8, 0x0c, 0x0d, 0x10, 0x15, 0x26 and 0x27
       3  the .map loader cannot produce them - mesh-3df-map.txt
          section 5 lists the kinds its flag bits select, and neither
          is among them
       4  neither has a g_spanTable record, so the software rasterizer
          could not draw one if it existed

     What DOES reference them is D3D_DrawScene, which routes both to
     D3D_SetBlendModeFlatAlpha and stamps alpha 0x80 into 0x44's flat
     colour when the device reports DoesAlphaFlatStippled. Their
     presence in the feature table proves nothing, since Screen3D_Open
     builds an entry for all 0x46 kinds by construction.

     So they look like DEAD KINDS - a hardware blend path with no
     geometry that selects it [inferred]. That is not proven: a kind
     written through a register rather than an immediate would escape
     method 1, and 0x44 and 0x45 are too common as small integers to
     search for as register loads. The honest position is that the
     obvious producers have been ruled out and the non-obvious ones
     have not been searched.

     o FOR A PORT: implement D3D_SetBlendModeFlatAlpha anyway - it is
       four render states - but do not spend effort reproducing
       whatever selects it until something is seen to.
   o Why kind 0x0c alone takes ONE / SRCCOLOR, and whether it is the
     only additive primitive in the game.
   o DAT_004e4f24 and DAT_004e4f28, adjusted at the top of
     D3D_DrawScene, are not identified. They look like a buffer count
     and a high-water mark [guess].
